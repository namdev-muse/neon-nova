How to Play

  • Move with WASD / arrow keys. The ship has momentum — it drifts, so steer early and commit.
  • Aim with the mouse and fire with click or Space. Your shots go where the crosshair points — nowhere else.
  • Dash with Shift to burst through danger. It has a short cooldown and a moment of invulnerability — spend it when the swarm closes in.
  • Chasers fly straight at you. Shooters hold their distance and fire bolts — watch the telegraph flash, then move. Splitters burst into two minis when they die.
  • Kills chain a combo — every kill within a few seconds raises the multiplier, up to x4. Taking a hit resets it.
  • Enemies drop gold energy — fly over it to collect. Energy is currency: spend it on ship upgrades between campaign sets and every 3 survival waves. Clear every wave to finish the set.
  • You have 3 shields. A hit costs one and stuns the ship; lose all three and the run ends. Standing still is how you die — the swarm comes from every side.
  • Your best score is saved per wave set — beat it. Clearing a set unlocks the next one; clearing Set 1 unlocks Survival, endless waves that never stop.
  • Campaign plays all 12 sets in a row — score, energy and upgrades carry across sets, and death ends the campaign. Survival is endless: how deep can you go?

Controls

  • WASD / arrows: move · Mouse: aim · Click / Space: fire
  • Shift: dash · P: pause · M: mute · R: restart
  • Touch: left stick moves, drag right side to aim and fire, DASH button dashes
  • Esc closes a panel · Enter confirms on menus
